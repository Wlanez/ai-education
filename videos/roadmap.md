## Status meanings

- **Not started** — no brief has passed review.
- **Brief** — the commercial brief is complete; demo is not yet reproducible.
- **Demo** — the scenario and evidence can be reproduced and reset.
- **Script** — English narration and screen directions are ready to rehearse.
- **Recorded** — long-form recording is complete; publication may still be pending.
- **Published** — long-form and derivatives are live and linked.
- **Reviewed** — outcome has been entered in the scorecard and lessons recorded.

Use — for a date or result not yet recorded. Use 0 only when the result was measured and was zero.
