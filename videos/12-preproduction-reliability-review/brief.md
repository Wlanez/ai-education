# Video 12 Brief — Full pre-production automation review

**Status:** Brief  
**Buyer:** Technical or operations leader preparing a business automation or agent for production.  
**Failure:** A pre-production review checks only the happy path and misses authorization, duplicate delivery, provider outage, stale data, and recovery behavior.  
**Business consequence:** In an appointment workflow, duplicate booking events may confirm two customers for one available slot. The business must cancel one appointment and handle the affected customer complaint.  
**Detection:** Run a scenario checklist and record expected versus observed outcome, signal, control, and owner for each case.  
**Control:** Use a scoped reliability review: workflow map, prioritized failure scenarios, evidence/log review, bounded controls, human approval where needed, and a written remediation order.  
**Evidence:** Show a synthetic workflow's happy path and selected failures, then summarize which controls passed, failed, or remain untested.  
**CTA:** Request a review conversation about an automation or AI agent already in development or production.

## Production note

Use synthetic examples, verify that the failure is reproducible, and limit claims to the evidence captured. Complete a demo plan and English script before recording.
