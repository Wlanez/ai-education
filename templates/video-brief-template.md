# Video Brief — [Number and working title]

**Status:** Not started  
**Target:** YouTube long-form (6–9 minutes), one 45–60 second Short, one LinkedIn post  
**Primary CTA:** Request a review conversation about an existing automation or AI agent.

## 1. Buyer

Which company and decision-maker will recognize this problem? What workflow is already in development or production?

## 2. Failure

What observable, realistic failure will the video introduce? State the normal path and the condition that breaks it.

## 3. Business consequence

What happens to a customer, record, payment, operation, cost, or trust when this failure occurs? Explain it in plain language.

## 4. Detection

How would the team know the failure happened? Name the signal, log, metric, alert, or reconciliation check.

## 5. Damage-limiting control

What bounded control changes the behavior? State its limit and any remaining risk.

## 6. Evidence

What repeatable before-and-after evidence will the viewer see? Include expected outcome and how to reset the demonstration.

## 7. One viewer action

Invite the viewer to request a review conversation about an existing automation. Do not ask them to share credentials, production payloads, or confidential details.

## Production gate

Keep the brief in **Not started** or **Brief** until all seven answers are concrete. Do not move it to Demo or Script if the buyer or business consequence is vague, the failure is not reproducible, the control is unbounded, or the evidence cannot support the claim.

## Safety and delivery notes

- Use synthetic or safely redacted data.
- Record setup, failure injection, reset, and expected evidence in demo-plan.md.
- Keep claims limited to what the demonstration proves.
- Use short English sentences, explain necessary technical terms, and time the read-through.
