- How many qualified calls took place, and what made them qualified?
- Which buyer problems appeared repeatedly?
- Which industries produced the strongest response?
- Which videos were linked to conversations or opportunities?
- Which review tasks could become a repeatable service?
- Is there enough evidence to narrow the audience or change the roadmap?
