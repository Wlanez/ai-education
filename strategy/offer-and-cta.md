# Offer and Call to Action

## One primary call to action

> Request a review conversation about an automation or AI agent that is already in development or production.

Use this same commercial action across videos. Educational prompts such as subscribing may appear after it, but do not replace or compete with it.

## Service path

1. **Fit conversation** — understand the workflow, failure, consequence, and whether a review is appropriate.
2. **Fixed-scope reliability review** — inspect an agreed workflow and its available evidence within a defined boundary.
3. **Written findings** — summarize observed failure modes, evidence gaps, and prioritized remediation options.
4. **Repair or controlled pilot** — implement an agreed improvement and verify it against the failure scenario.
5. **Optional operational support** — discuss monitoring and ongoing support only when the need is demonstrated.

## Review principles

- Agree scope, access, data handling, and deliverables before examining a system.
- Use the least sensitive information that can answer the question.
- Prefer synthetic or redacted examples for public demonstrations.
- Separate observed facts from hypotheses and recommendations.
- Do not promise zero failures or imply that one control solves every outage.
- Pricing is intentionally not fixed here; test the offer before standardizing it.

## CTA usage

At the end of each video, name the audience and invite a fit conversation about an existing automation. Do not add multiple commercial offers or ask viewers to send secrets or production data.
