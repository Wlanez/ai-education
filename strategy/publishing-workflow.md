# Publishing Workflow

## Weekly production cycle

1. **Select** the next failure from `videos/roadmap.md`.
2. **Brief** the buyer, failure, business consequence, detection, control, evidence, and CTA. Hold production if any answer is vague.
3. **Reproduce** the scenario with deterministic setup, synthetic data, failure injection, reset, and expected evidence.
4. **Script and rehearse** in English. Use short sentences, explain technical terms, and time the read-through for 6–9 minutes.
5. **Record** the healthy path, failure, consequence, control, and verification. Capture only evidence the demo can support.
6. **Review** the quality controls before publication, including confidentiality and claim checks.
7. **Publish** the long-form YouTube video. Produce one 45–60 second Short and one LinkedIn post from the same case.
8. **Measure** commercial outcomes in `metrics/scorecard.md`; distinguish zero from not measured.
9. **Learn** from qualified conversations and update future priorities only when evidence supports the change.

## Production gate

Do not mark a video ready to record until:

- Its brief answers all seven required questions.
- The failure can be repeated and reset.
- The business consequence is plain and specific.
- The control is proportional to the risk.
- The evidence supports each technical claim.
- All demo data are synthetic or safely redacted.
- The English script sounds natural when read aloud and meets the target duration.
- The video has one primary CTA for a review conversation.

## Roles and records

The creator owns the brief, demo, script, recording, and scorecard update. Keep decisions and reusable evidence in the video's folder. Do not commit credentials, production data, or confidential employer/client information.
