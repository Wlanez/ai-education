# Positioning

## Who this channel helps

Companies that already have a business automation or AI agent in development or production and need it to behave reliably when real conditions are messy.

## Core promise

> I help companies diagnose and stabilize automations and AI agents that work in a demo but fail in real operations.

Supporting message:

> Your automation works—until something unexpected happens. I help find and fix those failure points before they cost you customers, money, or trust.

## Editorial point of view

Start with an observable failure. Show the normal path, introduce a realistic fault, explain the business consequence, diagnose the cause, add a proportional control, then rerun the scenario and show evidence.

The video should make clear what changed and what the evidence proves. A tool is included only when it helps explain or control the business failure.

## Audience signals

Good-fit viewers may recognize one or more of these situations:

- Records are lost, duplicated, or processed more than once.
- Integrations fail without an alert.
- An AI agent returns unsupported answers or crosses a user's access boundary.
- Retries increase cost or make an incident worse.
- Sensitive actions run without human approval.
- Teams lack logs or trace evidence to investigate incidents.

## First 90 days

Use examples from multiple industries. Do not select one vertical until recorded conversations and scorecard evidence show that a focused vertical would help.

## Language and delivery

- Main video language: English.
- Delivery target: clear B2 English supported by a complete script.
- Long-form videos: 6–9 minutes.
- Short-form derivatives: 45–60 seconds.
- Use short sentences, explain necessary technical terms, and leave natural pauses.

## Boundaries

- Do not organize the channel around a vendor or tool.
- Do not publish general AI news or prompt collections detached from a business workflow.
- Do not expose confidential employer or client information.
- Do not claim a control works beyond the scenario and evidence shown.
- Views and subscriber growth support the work; qualified client calls are the primary outcome.
