# AI Education — Failure-First Content System

## Purpose

This repository supports a 90-day YouTube season designed to generate qualified conversations with companies whose business automations or AI agents fail under real operating conditions.

## Positioning

> I help companies diagnose and stabilize automations and AI agents that work in a demo but fail in real operations.

Each video starts with a visible failure, shows the business consequence, diagnoses the cause, adds a proportional control, and reruns the scenario with evidence.
